Every course aims at three things

METP is measured by what a leader knows, what they can do, and what they have come to value.

Head

Educated

The doctrines, the story of Scripture, and how to interpret and apply it faithfully.

Hand

Equipped

The practical work of ministry: preaching, counselling, resolving conflict, leading and mentoring.

Heart

Enriched

A growing intimacy with God, love for people, and reliance on the Holy Spirit in ministry.

Attendance

Classroom sessions are monitored as part of each student’s record.

Forty-eight outcomes

Linked to specific courses, but they can be demonstrated at any point in the program.

A short exam per course

Each of the twelve courses closes with a test of what was covered.

Two certificates, both worth having

GlobalEd Institute

METP Certificate

Awarded on completing all twelve courses and passing each exam. It references the GlobalEd Institute as part of Summit Pacific College, an accredited Bible college in Abbotsford, British Columbia.

Training partners who meet the partnership requirements can have their own logo added.

Re-Forma · endorsed by the WEA

Certificate of Ministry

For students who demonstrate all forty-eight learning outcomes, assessed against Re-Forma’s global standards for non-formal ministry training and endorsed by the World Evangelical Alliance.

Outcome assessments were added to METP in 2025.

METP is a non-formal program and does not carry academic credit. Any ministry credentials or advanced standing granted by another organisation or institution are at that body’s discretion.

For churches, denominations and colleges

Launch a METP training location

METP reaches most of its students through local partners. You bring the leaders and the facilitators; the GlobalEd Institute provides the curriculum, the training and the certificates.

Partnerships are active in more than twenty countries, and the curriculum has been translated into more than fifteen languages.

Who partners with METP

  • Local churches equipping people for ministry in their own congregation
  • Denominations using METP as entry-level training for pastors and church planters
  • Organisations training leaders from several denominational backgrounds
  • Bible colleges and seminaries running METP as an extension program

What the annual licence includes

  • The full METP curriculum and teaching materials
  • Student enrolment and progress tracking with the GlobalEd Institute
  • Certificates for every graduate, eligible for Re-Forma recognition
  • Guidance on organising the program for your context

The licence fee covers administration. Partners may set their own local student fees.

Choose a pace that suits your students

Every option keeps the twelve-hour minimum per course. Some outcome assessments may happen outside class time.

12 months

One course a month

A Friday evening and full Saturday each month, or three hours a week where students live nearby.

6 months

Two courses a month

Two three-hour classes a week for students who work and live close to the location.

3 months

One course a week

An intensive residential option that some partners use.

Of roughly 2.4 million Christian leaders worldwide, about one in ten has had theological training. The rest lead without it, held back by distance, cost and access.

METP launched in 2019 to close that gap: a structured, credible program that can be taught quickly, in any language, wherever a group of leaders can gather.

Mission

To strengthen churches globally by providing essential resources to equip ministry leaders with little or no access to formal theological training.

Vision

To see God’s kingdom advanced, the church multiplied, and communities flourishing as a result of trained, confident, and motivated Christian leaders.

Who stands behind METP

A non-formal program of the GlobalEd Institute, part of the Extension Studies department at Summit Pacific College.
