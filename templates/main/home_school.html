How admission works

Four stages, and you can see which one you are in at any time.

  1. 01

    Apply online

    Your child's details, your contact details and a little about their previous school. Save and come back if you need to.

  2. 02

    Send documents

    Photograph them with your phone. If anything is unclear we will tell you exactly what to send again.

  3. 03

    We review

    A named member of the admissions team handles your file. Expect an outcome within [__] working days.

  4. 04

    Offer and acceptance

    An offer, a place on the waiting list, or a clear reason. Accept by paying the registration fee and the place is held.

What to have ready

Gather these before you start and the form takes about fifteen minutes. Requirements differ slightly by grade, and the form will tell you which apply to you.

  • Certified birth certificate for your child
  • Your ID, passport or asylum permit
  • The most recent school report
  • Transfer letter from the previous school, where one applies
  • Immunisation record
  • Proof of address
  • Court order for custody or guardianship, where one applies

Returning next year

Towards the end of each year we email every family a private link to confirm their child's place for the year ahead. There is no new password to remember.

The link takes about five minutes and picks up where you left off if you are interrupted.

  1. 1 Confirm your details and update anything medical we should know
  2. 2 Choose subjects, where the grade requires it
  3. 3 Sign the fee agreement
  4. 4 Pay the deposit and the place is held

Lost your link, or it has expired? Contact the office and we will send a new one.
