Built for the pastor already doing the work

Most of the leaders we train were called into ministry long before anyone offered them a classroom. They are preaching every Sunday, sitting with grieving families on Tuesday, and keeping the lights on the rest of the week.

SNAP Uplift fits around that. Short courses you can work through on a phone, in your own time, with no deadline pushing you. Nothing to pay until you have finished and want the certificate in your hand.

  • Works on a low-cost phone and a slow connection
  • No card required — pay by bank transfer
  • Start and stop whenever ministry demands it

Four areas of ministry

Every course sits in one of these. Take them in any order, or work through the whole pathway from the beginning.

Preaching and teaching

How to study a passage, build a sermon around it, and hold a room while you deliver it.

Pastoral care

Sitting with people through grief, illness, conflict and marriage without carrying it all yourself.

Leading a church

Money, records, teams and governance. The unglamorous work that keeps a congregation standing.

Outreach and missions

Reaching the community around your building, and planting further than it.

Three steps, in order

  1. 01

  2. 02
