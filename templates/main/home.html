The Challenge

Most pastors lead without ever being trained to.

Across sub-Saharan Africa, millions of men and women carry the weight of leading churches, planting communities, and discipling entire villages — with no access to the theological foundations they need.

Geography isolates them. Economic pressure prevents travel. Bible colleges require years away from their congregations. The result: passionate, called leaders left without tools.

No access to Bible colleges

Rural pastors are often hours from the nearest city, and colleges cost more than a year's income.

Cannot leave their congregation

Many lead churches of 50–200 members who depend on them week to week. Absence isn't an option.

Existing resources aren't contextualised

Western curricula rarely speak to the realities of African church life, culture, or context.

This platform changes that

Courses delivered online, in their own time, from any phone — built specifically for African ministry leaders.

About Judea Harvest

26 years of equipping
African ministry

Since 1999, Judea Harvest has mobilised, equipped, and resourced ministry leaders across Africa — so that their communities may experience the fullness of Jesus Christ.

Through programmes like John Journey (soul-winning and church-planting training), Tent Churches, SNAP Churches, and the Untrained Pastor initiative, we have seen almost 36,000 new churches established in 40 countries. This digital platform is our next step: bringing the same equipping online, to every leader with a phone.
