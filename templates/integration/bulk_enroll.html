How Bulk Enrollment Works

Follow these steps for successful enrollment

1

Select a Course

Choose which course to enroll students into

2

Choose Filter Criteria

Filter students by region, education level, church size, or enroll all verified students

3

Review Preview

See how many students match your criteria before proceeding

4

Submit Enrollment

Students are enrolled in the Django system with pending sync status

5

Sync to Moodle

Go to Sync Dashboard and click "Enrollments" to push changes to Moodle

Important Notes

• Only verified and active students will be enrolled

• Students already enrolled in the course will be skipped automatically

• Enrollments are created with pending sync status until pushed to Moodle
