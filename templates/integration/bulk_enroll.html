How Bulk Enrollment Works

  1. Select a course - Choose which course to enroll students in
  2. Choose filter criteria - Filter students by region, education level, church size, or enroll all verified students
  3. Review preview - See how many students match your criteria
  4. Enroll - Students are enrolled in the Django system with pending sync status
  5. Sync to Moodle - Go to sync dashboard and click "Enrollments" to push changes to Moodle

Note: Only verified and active students will be enrolled. Students already enrolled in the course will be skipped.
