Enrollment Reconciliation

Clean up stale access and synchronize enrollment status

What does this do?

  • Removes Moodle access for users who no longer have active enrollments
  • Adds missing enrollments for users with active products
  • Deactivates Moodle accounts with no active courses
  • Reactivates suspended accounts when users purchase new courses

Use this when: Orders or products have been deleted and users still have Moodle access

{% csrf_token %}

Important Notes

  • This process can take 5-10 minutes for systems with many users
  • The page will reload automatically when complete
  • Users will be immediately logged out of Moodle if access is removed
  • Check the sync history after completion for detailed results
